research behind the sketch

  1. lu et al., 2003 / sauerkrautspontaneous, ~18°C, 2.2–2.3% salt. LAB reached ~10⁹ CFU/ml on day 3 and ~10⁷ by day 60; sugar changed little after day 30; final pH ~3.5.
  2. tlais et al., 2022 / sauerkrautspontaneous, 15°C. LAB rose during the first four days, then stayed high through day 42. pH reached 3.93 on day 7 and 3.36 on day 42. evidence that the late LAB decline is highly variable.
  3. boylu et al., 2023 / oyster mushroomsspontaneous, sealed pouches, 21–22°C; 2% salt plus 1% sucrose. the untreated mushroom arm fell from pH 6.35 to 3.96 in eight days. an endpoint check, not a complete kinetic curve.
  4. nguyen & nguyen, 2025 / kimchithe non-starter arm at 20°C reached pH 4.1–4.2 in ~66 hours. only the spontaneous arm informs this sketch; the starter treatments are excluded.
  5. ropelewska et al., 2024 / sweet pepperspontaneous, 20°C, 3.5% covering brine. tissue sugar concentration fell to ~23% of the initial value by day 56. this is not a whole-bag sugar mass balance.
  6. alberto et al., 2013 / peppersnatural-fermentation controls in supplemented media, including 22°C. maximum viable counts occurred between days 2 and 5; subsequent counts declined or remained stable.
