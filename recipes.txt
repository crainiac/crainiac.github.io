Sprisae Pork
~ 1/4 cup Sprite
~ 1/4 cup orange juice
~ 1/3 cup soy sauce
~ 1 tbsp lime juice
~ 1 tbsp sesame oil
~ 1/2 cup gochujang
~ 1 tbsp brown sugar
~ 6 cloves garlic
~ 2 green onions
~ 2 jalapenos, stemmed
~ 2 lbs pork belly, sliced
Blend all ingredients besides pork belly and marinate for 2+ hours.

Paocai Verde
~ 1 Asian pear, chopped
~ 6 shiitakes, sliced
~ 8 tomatillos, chopped
~ 1 leek, chopped
~ 1/4 large daikon, chopped
~ 8 serranos, chopped
~ 8 green onions, chopped
~ 1 huge knob ginger, chopped
~ 8 cloves garlic, minced
~ 1/4 cup whole huajiao
~ 1 tbsp coriander seeds, lightly crushed
~ 8 cups fresh sprouts (alfalfa, broccoli, radish, lentil, mung), chopped
Freeze all ingredients besides sprouts for 24+ hours. Add frozen ingredients on top of sprouts in a vacuum seal bag. Weigh everything; subtract bag weight. Add 2% pickling salt by weight. Vacuum seal and ferment for 16 days. Works nicely as a topping for tuna sashimi.

Marie Clairevoie Mix
~ 3 large beefsteak tomatoes
~ 14 cherry tomatoes
~ 1 cup Campbell's 100% Tomato Juice
~ 8 celery ribs
~ 2 large red bell peppers, stemmed
~ 1/2 cup tomato bouillon concentrate (3 parts Knorr Tomato with Chicken Granulated Bouillon, 1 part boiling water; cool before blending)
~ 1/4 cup lemon juice
~ 1/4 cup lime juice
~ 1/3 cup Bubbie's dill pickle juice
~ 1/2 cup Worcestershire
~ 3 tbsp Bragg Liquid Aminos
~ 1/4 cup Sriracha
~ 1/3 cup Bubbie's prepared horseradish
Blend and freeze solid. Thaw in a fine-mesh strainer without pressing (up to 2 hours at room temperature, then finish thawing in the fridge).

Marie Clairevoie
~ 5 oz Marie Clairevoie Mix
~ 1 1/2 oz St. George Aqua Perfecta Basil Eau de Vie
~ 1/4 oz lemon juice
~ 1/4 oz lime juice
~ 1 splash Fever-Tree Premium Club Soda, cold
~ 1 tsp lemon pepper salt (5 parts kosher salt, 2 parts dehydrated lemon, 1 part black pepper)
~ 1 lemon wedge
~ 1 cherry tomato
Grind dehydrated lemon and pepper, then mix with salt. Rub lemon wedge around rim and discard; coat rim with lemon pepper salt. Fill glass with ice, add everything besides club soda and cherry tomato, and stir. Top with club soda and garnish with skewered cherry tomato.

Preserved Serrano Lemon
~ 8 lemons, quartered
~ 4 serranos, stemmed & split
~ 2 bay leaves
~ 1 tbsp huajiao, lightly crushed
~ 1 tbsp coriander seeds, lightly crushed
Mix all ingredients in a vacuum seal bag. Add 2% pickling salt by weight. Vacuum seal and ferment for 2 weeks.

Saltyearth
~ 1 1/2 oz Montelobos Espadin
~ 3/4 oz Preserved Serrano Lemon (juice only)
~ 1/2 oz Midori
Shake with ice and strain.

Khoresh Bademjanasu Dengaku
~ 2 lb Asian eggplant, halved
~ 1 lb cherry tomatoes
~ 1 g saffron, crushed
~ 1 lb lamb shoulder, bone-in
~ 1 tbsp sesame oil
Add half of the eggplant and tomato with 2% pickling salt by weight to a vacuum bag and seal. Ferment for 3 weeks at room temperature, reserve 1/2 cup of brine, then puree. Reduce ~3/4 cup puree over medium-low heat, stirring often, until paste-like; reserve the rest. Marinate lamb in reserved brine 2+ hours, then slow-cook half-covered in water on low until tender, blooming the saffron near the end of cooking in hot reserved stock. Score the other half of eggplant, brush it with sesame oil, and roast cut-side down at 425 F until tender, about 20 min. Spread reduced puree over the roasted eggplant and broil cut-side up on high until browned. Fry the other half of tomato in a little sesame oil until blistered and beginning to burst. After turning off the slow cooker and reserving the lamb, mix saffron into the lamb stock and add unreduced puree until desired stew thickness is reached. Nestle the lamb, glazed eggplant, and fried tomato into the stew. Serve with rice.
