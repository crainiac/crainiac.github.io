Sprisae Pork
~ 1/4 cup Sprite
~ 1/4 cup orange juice
~ 1/3 cup soy sauce
~ 1 tbsp lime juice
~ 1 tbsp sesame oil
~ 1/2 cup gochujang
~ 1 tbsp brown sugar
~ 6 cloves garlic
~ 2 green onions
~ 2 jalapenos, stemmed
~ 2 lb pork belly, sliced
Blend all ingredients besides pork and marinate for a few hours.

Cherry Miso Chuck
~ 1/2 cup Cherry Coke
~ 1/2 cup orange juice
~ 1/4 cup soy sauce
~ 1/4 cup red miso paste
~ 6 cloves garlic
~ 1 red onion
~ 2 habaneros, stemmed
~ 2 lb chuck steak
Blend all ingredients besides steak and slow-cook on low ~8 hours.

Amakoji
~1 cup koji rice
~1 cup water
Hold at 330 kelvins for ~12 hours (e.g., sous vide). Chill.

Hakko Shojin-eki
~ 6 shiitakes
~ 1 strip kombu
Freeze shiitakes overnight. Vacuum seal with kombu and 2% salt by weight. Ferment at room temperature ~8 days. Strain.

Ajitama
~ 6 eggs
~ 1/2 cup katsuo dashi
~ 1/2 cup Amakoji
~ 1/4 cup nama shoyu
~ 1 tbsp Hakko Shojin-eki
~ 1 tbsp red miso paste
Blend all ingredients besides eggs. Hold eggs from fridge at 360 kelvins for 6 minutes (e.g., sous vide). Chill in ice bath and peel. Marinate in remaining ingredients ~8 hours, refrigerated, mixing occasionally.

Haburajiao
~ 20 habaneros, stemmed
~ 1 huge knob horseradish, peeled
~ 1 beefsteak tomato, cored
~ 1 red bell pepper, cored
~ 4 cloves garlic, crushed
~ 1 bunch fresh basil
~ 2 tbsp huajiao
Blend habaneros, horseradish, tomato, and bell pepper. Freeze overnight. Vacuum seal with remaining ingredients, add 2% salt by weight, and ferment at room temperature for a few weeks. Blend and bottle.

Funky Mary Mix
~ 3 large beefsteak tomatoes, cored
~ 14 cherry tomatoes
~ 8 celery ribs
~ 2 large red bell peppers, cored
~ 4 habaneros, stemmed
~ 6 cloves garlic
~ 1 huge knob fresh horseradish, peeled
~ 1 bunch dill
Blend everything besides dill. Freeze overnight. Vacuum seal with dill and 2% pickling salt by weight; ferment at room temperature ~2 weeks. Strain. Save solids.

Funky Mary Salt
~ 2 parts dehydrated Funky Mary solids
~ 5 parts kosher salt
Dehydrate solids until brittle. Grind and mix with salt.

Funky Mary
~ 4 oz Funky Mary Mix
~ 1 1/2 oz St. George Aqua Perfecta Basil Eau de Vie
~ 3/4 oz Campbell's 100% Tomato Juice
~ 1/4 oz tomato bouillon concentrate (3 parts Knorr Tomato with Chicken Granulated Bouillon, 1 part boiling water, by weight; stir vigorously, refrigerate)
~ 1 tsp Funky Mary Salt
~ 1 lemon wedge
Rub lemon wedge around rim; coat rim with Funky Mary Salt. Fill glass with ice and stir with liquids. Garnish with lemon wedge.

Preserved Serrano Lemon Juice
~ 8 lemons
~ 4 serranos, stemmed & split
~ 2 bay leaves
~ 1 tbsp huajiao
~ 1 tbsp coriander seeds
Blend lemons' zest and juice and freeze overnight. Vacuum seal with remaining ingredients and 2% pickling salt by weight; ferment ~3 weeks. Strain.

Salty Cruise
~ 1 1/2 oz Montelobos Espadin
~ 3/4 oz Preserved Serrano Lemon Juice
~ 1/2 oz Midori
Shake with ice and strain.

Aji Verde
~ 1/2 cup mayonnaise
~ 1/4 cup greek yogurt
~ 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
~ 1 tbsp lime juice
~ 4 jalapeños, stemmed
~ 1 cup fresh cilantro leaves
~ 2 green onions, green parts only
~ 4 cloves garlic
~ 1/2 tsp salt
~ 1/4 tsp black pepper
Blend and refrigerate.

Aji Verde Chicken
~ 1/3 cup soy sauce
~ 2 tbsp lime juice
~ 5 cloves garlic
~ 2 tsp cumin
~ 1 tsp paprika
~ 1/2 tsp dried oregano
~ 1 pinch black pepper
~ 2 lb chicken thighs
Blend all ingredients besides chicken and slow-cook on low for 6-8 hours. Serve with Aji Verde.

Paocai Verde
~ 1 Asian pear, chopped
~ 6 shiitakes, sliced
~ 8 tomatillos, chopped
~ 1 leek, chopped
~ 1/4 large daikon, chopped
~ 8 serranos, chopped
~ 8 green onions, chopped
~ 1 huge knob ginger, chopped
~ 8 cloves garlic, minced
~ 1/4 cup whole huajiao
~ 1 tbsp coriander seeds, lightly crushed
~ 8 cups fresh sprouts (alfalfa, broccoli, radish, lentil, mung), chopped
Freeze all ingredients besides sprouts for 24+ hours. Add frozen ingredients on top of sprouts in a vacuum seal bag. Weigh everything; subtract bag weight. Add 2% pickling salt by weight. Vacuum seal and ferment 2+ weeks.

Khoresh Bademjanasu Dengaku
~ 2 lb Asian eggplant, halved
~ 1 lb cherry tomatoes
~ 1 g saffron, crushed
~ 1 lb lamb shoulder, bone-in
Vacuum-seal half of the eggplant with 2% pickling salt by weight; repeat separately with half of the tomato. Ferment each for a week at room temperature. Puree eggplant ferment. Strain tomato ferment, reserving solids and brine. Marinate lamb in tomato brine overnight, then slow-cook half-covered in water on low until tender. Near the end, skim rendered lamb fat and reserve; use some lamb stock to bloom saffron. Score the unfermented eggplant, brush with lamb fat, and roast cut-side down at 425 F until tender, about 20 min. Turn cut-side up, pack fermented tomato solids into the scoring, and broil on high until browned. Fry the unfermented tomato in lamb fat until blistered and beginning to burst. Remove lamb and turn off slow cooker. Pour bloomed saffron into the lamb stock; mix in eggplant puree to taste. Add lamb, glazed eggplant, and fried tomato.
