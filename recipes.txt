Sprisae Pork
~ 1/4 cup Sprite
~ 1/4 cup orange juice
~ 1/3 cup soy sauce
~ 1 tbsp lime juice
~ 1 tbsp sesame oil
~ 1/2 cup gochujang
~ 1 tbsp brown sugar
~ 6 cloves garlic
~ 2 green onions
~ 2 jalapenos, stemmed
~ 2 lbs pork belly, sliced
Blend all ingredients besides pork belly and marinate for 2+ hours.

Paocai Verde
~ 1 Asian pear, chopped
~ 6 shiitakes
~ 8 tomatillos, chopped
~ 1 leek, chopped
~ 1 small daikon, chopped
~ 6 serranos, chopped
~ 8 green onions, chopped
~ 1 huge knob ginger, chopped
~ 8 cloves garlic, crushed
~ 1/4 cup whole huajiao
~ 1 tbsp coriander seeds, lightly crushed
~ 4 cups fresh sprouts (alfalfa, broccoli, radish, lentil, mung), chopped
Freeze all ingredients besides sprouts for 24+ hours. Add frozen ingredients on top of sprouts in a vacuum seal bag. Weigh everything; subtract bag weight. Add 2% pickling salt by weight. Vacuum seal and ferment for 16 days.

Marie Clairevoie Mix
~ 3 large beefsteak tomatoes
~ 14 cherry tomatoes
~ 1 cup Campbell's 100% Tomato Juice
~ 8 celery ribs
~ 2 large red bell peppers, stemmed
~ 1/2 cup tomato bouillon concentrate (3 parts Knorr Tomato with Chicken Granulated Bouillon, 1 part boiling water; cool before blending)
~ 1/4 cup lemon juice
~ 1/4 cup lime juice
~ 1/3 cup Bubbie's dill pickle juice
~ 1/2 cup Worcestershire
~ 3 tbsp Bragg Liquid Aminos
~ 1/4 cup Sriracha
~ 1/3 cup Bubbie's prepared horseradish
Blend and freeze solid. Thaw in a fine-mesh strainer without pressing (up to 2 hours at room temperature, then finish thawing in the fridge).

Marie Clairevoie
~ 4 oz Marie Clairevoie Mix
~ 1/2 oz Plymouth Gin Navy Strength
~ 1/2 oz St. George Aqua Perfecta Basil Eau de Vie
~ 1/2 oz St-Germain Elderflower Liqueur
~ 1/4 oz lemon juice
~ 1/4 oz lime juice
~ 1 oz cold seltzer
~ 1 tsp lemon pepper salt (5 parts kosher salt, 2 parts dehydrated lemon, 1 part black pepper)
~ 1 cherry tomato
~ 1 Bubbie's dill pickle chip
~ 1 lemon wedge
Grind dehydrated lemon and pepper, then mix with salt. Rub lemon wedge around rim and coat with lemon pepper salt. Fill glass with ice, add everything besides seltzer and garnishes, and stir. Top with seltzer and garnish with skewered cherry tomato, dill pickle, and lemon wedge.
