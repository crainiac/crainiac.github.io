Sprisae Pork
~ 1/4 cup Sprite
~ 1/4 cup orange juice
~ 1/3 cup soy sauce
~ 1 tbsp lime juice
~ 1 tbsp sesame oil
~ 1/2 cup gochujang
~ 1 tbsp brown sugar
~ 6 cloves garlic
~ 2 green onions
~ 2 jalapenos, stemmed
~ 2 lbs pork belly, sliced
Blend all ingredients besides pork belly and marinate for 2+ hours.

Paocai Verde
~ 1 Asian pear, chopped
~ 6 shiitakes, sliced
~ 8 tomatillos, chopped
~ 1 leek, chopped
~ 1/4 large daikon, chopped
~ 8 serranos, chopped
~ 8 green onions, chopped
~ 1 huge knob ginger, chopped
~ 8 cloves garlic, minced
~ 1/4 cup whole huajiao
~ 1 tbsp coriander seeds, lightly crushed
~ 8 cups fresh sprouts (alfalfa, broccoli, radish, lentil, mung), chopped
Freeze all ingredients besides sprouts for 24+ hours. Add frozen ingredients on top of sprouts in a vacuum seal bag. Weigh everything; subtract bag weight. Add 2% pickling salt by weight. Vacuum seal and ferment for 16 days. Works nicely as a topping for tuna sashimi.

Funky Mary Mix
~ 3 large beefsteak tomatoes, cored
~ 14 cherry tomatoes
~ 8 celery ribs
~ 2 large red bell peppers, stemmed
~ 4 habaneros, stemmed
~ 6 cloves garlic
~ 1 huge knob fresh horseradish, peeled
~ 1/2 bunch basil
~ 1 bunch dill
~ 1 cup Campbell's 100% Tomato Juice
~ 1/4 cup tomato bouillon concentrate (3 parts Knorr Tomato with Chicken Granulated Bouillon, 1 part boiling water, by weight; stir vigorously, refrigerate)
Blend everything through horseradish. Freeze overnight. Vacuum seal with basil, dill, and 2% pickling salt by weight; ferment at room temperature ~1 week. Strain. Stir remaining ingredients into liquid. Save solids.

Funky Mary Salt
~ 2 parts dehydrated Funky Mary solids
~ 5 parts kosher salt
Dehydrate solids. Grind and mix with salt.

Funky Mary
~ 5 oz Funky Mary Mix
~ 1 1/2 oz St. George Aqua Perfecta Basil Eau de Vie
~ 1/4 oz lemon juice
~ 1/4 oz lime juice
~ 1 splash Fever-Tree Premium Club Soda, cold
~ 1 tsp Funky Mary Salt
~ 1 lemon wedge
~ 1 cherry tomato
Rub lemon wedge around rim and discard; coat rim with Funky Mary Salt. Fill glass with ice, add everything besides club soda and cherry tomato, and stir. Top with club soda and garnish with skewered cherry tomato.

Preserved Serrano Lime
~ 15 limes, halved
~ 6 serranos, stemmed & split
~ 2 bay leaves
~ 1 tbsp huajiao
~ 1 tbsp coriander seeds
Vacuum seal with 2% pickling salt by weight and ferment ~1 week.

Salty Cruise
~ 1 1/2 oz Montelobos Espadin
~ 1/2 oz Preserved Serrano Lime juice
~ 1/2 oz Midori
Shake with ice and strain.

Khoresh Bademjanasu Dengaku
~ 2 lb Asian eggplant, halved
~ 1 lb cherry tomatoes
~ 1 g saffron, crushed
~ 1 lb lamb shoulder, bone-in
Vacuum-seal half of the eggplant with 2% pickling salt by weight; repeat separately with half of the tomato. Ferment each for a week at room temperature. Puree eggplant ferment. Strain tomato ferment, reserving solids and brine. Marinate lamb in tomato brine overnight, then slow-cook half-covered in water on low until tender. Near the end, skim rendered lamb fat and reserve; use some lamb stock to bloom saffron. Score the unfermented eggplant, brush with lamb fat, and roast cut-side down at 425 F until tender, about 20 min. Turn cut-side up, pack fermented tomato solids into the scoring, and broil on high until browned. Fry the unfermented tomato in lamb fat until blistered and beginning to burst. Remove lamb and turn off slow cooker. Pour bloomed saffron into the lamb stock; mix in eggplant puree to taste. Add lamb, glazed eggplant, and fried tomato.
