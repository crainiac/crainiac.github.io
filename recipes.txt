Hakko Shojin Dashi
~ 15 shiitakes
~ 5 g kombu
Freeze shiitakes overnight. Vacuum seal with kombu and 2% salt by weight. Ferment at room temperature ~8 days. Strain. Try some drops on chutoro.

Shiitake Shio Koji
~ 1 cup dried donko
~ 1 cup dried rice koji
~ 1 1/2 cups water
~ 5 g kombu
Soak donko and kombu in water overnight, refrigerated. Remove kombu; mince donko and return to soaking liquid. Heat to 350 K; rest covered ~10 minutes, then simmer ~5 minutes. Replace evaporated water. Cool. Mix in koji and 11% salt by weight. Hold at 333 K ~8 hours (e.g., sous vide), stirring occasionally. Refrigerate.

Amakoji
~ 1 cup dried rice koji
~ 1 cup water
Hold at 333 K ~8 hours (e.g., sous vide), stirring occasionally for even water distribution. Refrigerate.

Clarified Processed Supertomato
~ 500 parts Processed Supertomato, by weight
~ 1 part Telephone Agar-Agar, by weight
Whisk agar into half of the Supertomato; bring to a boil and simmer ~2 minutes. Whisk in remaining Supertomato. Chill until gelled. Break gel into curds and gently squeeze through cheesecloth. Refrigerate.

Khoresh Bademjanasu Dengaku
~ 2 lb Asian eggplant, halved
~ 1 lb cherry tomatoes
~ 1 g saffron, crushed
~ 1 lb lamb shoulder, bone-in
Vacuum-seal half of the eggplant with 2% salt by weight; repeat separately with half of the tomato. Ferment each for a week at room temperature. Puree eggplant ferment. Strain tomato ferment, reserving solids and brine. Marinate lamb in tomato brine overnight, then slow-cook half-covered in water on low until tender. Near the end, skim rendered lamb fat and reserve; use some lamb stock to bloom saffron. Score the unfermented eggplant, brush with lamb fat, and roast cut-side down at 490 K until tender, about 20 min. Turn cut-side up, pack fermented tomato solids into the scoring, and broil on high until browned. Fry the unfermented tomato in lamb fat until blistered and beginning to burst. Remove lamb and turn off slow cooker. Pour bloomed saffron into the lamb stock; mix in eggplant puree to taste. Add lamb, glazed eggplant, and fried tomato.
