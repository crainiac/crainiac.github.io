Pickled Sprouts
~ 7 cups fresh sprouts (alfalfa, broccoli, radish, lentil, mung)
~ 3 tbsp lemon zest, avoiding pith
~ 1 huge knob ginger, peeled
~ 2 medium carrots
~ 2 tbsp coriander seeds
~ 1 tbsp cumin seeds
~ 4 habaneros
~ 1 ripe mango, peeled and seeded
~ 1 tsp green cardamom seeds
~ 1 tbsp fennel seeds
~ 1 tsp allspice berries
~ 1 tbsp nigella seeds
Blend ginger, carrots, and habaneros. Freeze overnight. Vacuum seal with remaining ingredients and 2% salt by weight. Ferment at room temperature ~3 weeks.

~ 16 serranos, stemmed
~ 1 bunch cilantro
~ 1 large handful epazote, tough stems removed
~ 2 large roasted poblanos, stemmed and seeded
~ 1/2 white onion
~ 1/2 cup unsalted pepitas, lightly toasted
~ 1/4 cup white sesame seeds, lightly toasted
~ 8 cloves garlic, crushed
~ 4 tbsp lime zest, avoiding pith
~ 4 tbsp coriander seeds

Hakko Shojin Dashi
~ 15 shiitakes
~ 5 g kombu
Freeze shiitakes overnight. Vacuum seal with kombu and 2% salt by weight. Ferment at room temperature ~8 days. Strain. Try some drops on chutoro.

Shio Koji
~ 1 cup koji rice
~ 1 cup water
Mix with 11% salt by total weight and cover at room temperature ~2 weeks, stirring occasionally. Refrigerate.

Amakoji
~ 1 cup koji rice
~ 1 cup water
Hold at 330 K for ~12 hours (e.g., sous vide). Refrigerate.

Amakoji Ajitama

~ 1/2 cup katsuo dashi
~ 1/2 cup Amakoji
~ 1/4 cup nama shoyu
~ 1 tbsp Hakko Shojin Dashi
~ 1 tbsp red miso paste
Blend all ingredients besides eggs. Cover eggs in blend and marinate ~12 hours, refrigerated.

Haburajiao
~ 20 habaneros, stemmed
~ 1 huge knob horseradish, peeled
~ 1 beefsteak tomato, cored
~ 1 red bell pepper, cored
~ 4 cloves garlic, crushed
~ 1 bunch fresh basil
~ 2 tbsp huajiao
Blend everything through bell pepper. Freeze overnight. Vacuum seal with remaining ingredients and 2% salt by weight. Ferment at room temperature ~1 month. Blend and refrigerate.

Processed Supertomato
~ 4 parts Campbell's 100% Tomato Juice, by weight
~ 1 part Knorr Tomato with Chicken Granulated Bouillon, by weight
Heat 1 part of the Campbell's until steaming. Stir in Knorr vigorously until combined. Cool and blend with the remaining Campbell's. Refrigerate.

Funky Mary Mix
~ 3 large beefsteak tomatoes, cored
~ 14 cherry tomatoes
~ 8 celery ribs
~ 2 large red bell peppers, cored
~ 4 habaneros, stemmed
~ 6 cloves garlic
~ 1 huge knob fresh horseradish, peeled
~ 1 bunch dill
Blend everything besides dill. Freeze overnight. Vacuum seal with dill and 2% salt by weight; ferment at room temperature ~10 days. Strain. Save solids.

Funky Mary
~ 4 oz Funky Mary Mix
~ 1 1/2 oz Aalborg Jubilaeums
~ 1 oz Processed Supertomato
~ 1 tsp Funky Mary Salt (2 parts dehydrated Funky Mary solids, 5 parts Diamond Crystal Kosher Salt)
~ 1 lemon wedge
Rub lemon wedge around rim; coat rim with Funky Mary Salt. Fill glass with ice and stir with liquids. Garnish with lemon wedge.

Clarified Processed Tomato
~ 500 parts Campbell's 100% Tomato Juice, by weight
~ 1 part Telephone Agar-Agar, by weight
Whisk agar into half of the juice; bring to a boil and simmer ~2 minutes. Whisk in remaining juice. Chill until gelled. Break gel into curds and gently squeeze through cheesecloth. Refrigerate.

Paocai Verde
~ 1 Asian pear, chopped
~ 6 shiitakes, sliced
~ 8 tomatillos, chopped
~ 1 leek, chopped
~ 1/4 large daikon, chopped
~ 8 serranos, chopped
~ 8 green onions, chopped
~ 1 huge knob ginger, chopped
~ 8 cloves garlic, minced
~ 1/4 cup huajiao
~ 1 tbsp coriander seeds, lightly crushed
~ 8 cups fresh sprouts (alfalfa, broccoli, radish, lentil, mung), chopped
Freeze all ingredients besides sprouts for 24+ hours. Add frozen ingredients on top of sprouts in a vacuum seal bag. Weigh everything; subtract bag weight. Add 2% salt by weight. Vacuum seal and ferment 2+ weeks.

Khoresh Bademjanasu Dengaku
~ 2 lb Asian eggplant, halved
~ 1 lb cherry tomatoes
~ 1 g saffron, crushed
~ 1 lb lamb shoulder, bone-in
Vacuum-seal half of the eggplant with 2% salt by weight; repeat separately with half of the tomato. Ferment each for a week at room temperature. Puree eggplant ferment. Strain tomato ferment, reserving solids and brine. Marinate lamb in tomato brine overnight, then slow-cook half-covered in water on low until tender. Near the end, skim rendered lamb fat and reserve; use some lamb stock to bloom saffron. Score the unfermented eggplant, brush with lamb fat, and roast cut-side down at 490 K until tender, about 20 min. Turn cut-side up, pack fermented tomato solids into the scoring, and broil on high until browned. Fry the unfermented tomato in lamb fat until blistered and beginning to burst. Remove lamb and turn off slow cooker. Pour bloomed saffron into the lamb stock; mix in eggplant puree to taste. Add lamb, glazed eggplant, and fried tomato.
